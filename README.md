## 📖 Overview

The **Luxury Automobile Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution built in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze luxury vehicle sales, revenue trends, pricing, and performance metrics.

The dashboard transforms raw automobile sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive visualizations, KPIs, and advanced DAX calculations, enabling stakeholders to make informed decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall automobile sales performance
- Monitor revenue trends over time
- Identify top-performing vehicle brands and models
- Compare revenue across body types
- Evaluate vehicle specifications
- Analyze regional sales distribution
- Understand customer buying preferences

# 📌 Key Insights

- Mercedes generated the highest overall revenue.
- SUVs contribute the largest share of vehicle sales.
- Revenue shows a strong upward trend over time.
- Automatic transmission dominates total revenue.
- A positive relationship exists between horsepower and selling price.
- Petrol vehicles account for the majority of total sales.
- Revenue distribution varies significantly across different U.S. states.
